Beat Tutor

Load any song from your phone. Beat Tutor listens to it, turns its beats and notes into taps, and coaches you through them.

  • ListenIt finds every beat and note in the track and works out the tempo.
  • TapNotes fall down four lanes. Hit each one as it lands on the line.
  • LearnSlow the song down, or use Learn mode so the music waits for you.

Works with MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG and FLAC. Load a MIDI file instead and you get the song's exact notes. Your music never leaves this device.
